

## 0.59.0 — Module 38 (Report Modules), slice 7: the GROUP report
Owner-approved on 2026-11-22 (all six decisions, with clarifications). The owner reported all tests passing locally for 0.57.0 and 0.58.0 (no counts given).
- **Implemented:** `GET /api/v1/reports/group` (JSON), `/group/csv`, `/group/xlsx`. Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), `groupId` (REQUIRED) and for the JSON `page`/`pageSize` (standard pagination of the STUDENTS: default 20, maximum 100).
- **Content:** the group header (name, school, learning mode, status, the current Teacher's name only, never a phone number); the roster of EVERY student assigned to the group at any time inside their own effective window (§67), from the authoritative assignment history, with or without activity, ARCHIVED/INACTIVE students included for the time they were in the group and never with a status; per student attendance, memorization and revision counts, evaluation count with the per-category count and mean (no overall score) and exam counts (examined, absent, total; no exam score); `totals` over the COMPLETE roster; and, for the Organization Manager and the School Manager only, the Teacher's attendance (present, absent, total). No subscription, payment or other financial data for any role.
- **Reuse:** the four learning figures are the existing ATTENDANCE, MEMORIZATION, REVISION and EVALUATION reports called unchanged for the group (same authorization, scope, effective start and Teacher restrictions); exam counts use the existing Module 30 student contract (0.57.0). The group is proven inside the scope before anything is read (a group outside it and one that does not exist: the same 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN`); a Teacher only for a group they currently teach. Parent, Collector, MAIN_ADMIN, geographic managers and Student: 403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`. No Module 12 permission added. A Teacher gets no Teacher-attendance section because the existing ATTENDANCE report shows a Teacher students only.
- **Files:** XLSX one worksheet per section (Attendance, Memorization, Revision, Evaluations, Exams (counts) and, for the two managers, Teacher attendance) with a row per student and a totals row; CSV one table (`section, student, group, item, measure, value`) with a short `Group` section first. Both hold every eligible student, not just a JSON page. PDF remains deferred.

## 0.60.0 — Module 38 (Report Modules), slice 8: the TEACHER report
Owner-approved on 2026-11-23 (Option A, with the mandatory multi-school scope clarification). The owner has not reported results for 0.59.0 (the Group report), so 0.59.0 is not claimed verified.
- **Implemented:** `GET /api/v1/reports/teacher` (JSON), `/teacher/csv`, `/teacher/xlsx`. Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), `teacherId` (REQUIRED) and for the JSON `page`/`pageSize` (standard pagination of the taught-group entries: default 20, maximum 100).
- **Multi-school scope (the central rule):** everything is limited to the requester's AUTHORIZED SCHOOLS (Organization Manager: the schools of their organization(s); School Manager: all the schools they actively manage), not the Teacher's whole employment. Every group read receives only those school ids; schools, groups, attendance, students and statistics of any other school or organization are never read, counted, paginated, listed, named or exported (JSON, `meta`, XLSX, CSV alike). Each manager is independent; a Teacher who works in A, B and C is seen by the manager of A through A only.
- **Authorization:** unchanged Module 37 availability matrix (TEACHER report: Organization Manager and School Manager only). A Teacher cannot run it, even for themselves; Parent, Collector, MAIN_ADMIN, geographic managers and Student: 403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`. The Teacher must be currently linked to an authorized school or have taught a group of one at some time (a Teacher who left a school keeps their history there); a Teacher outside that and one that does not exist get the same 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN` before any report data is read. No Module 12 permission added; the matrix is not modified beyond its documentation rows.
- **Content (Option A):** header = the Teacher's name and the authorized schools they are currently linked to (no phone, no address). `groups` = one entry per stint in which the Teacher taught a group of an authorized school, from the groups' authoritative history (first teacher at creation, TEACHER_CHANGED, archiving; never the current owner): group, school, learning mode, `taughtFrom`, `taughtTo`, `stillTeaching`, the Teacher's own attendance (present, absent, total) and the GROUP report's figures (roster size, attendance, memorization, revision, evaluation counts with per-category means, exam counts), all restricted to exactly the stint's dates. Option B (records the Teacher personally made) is not built. No score, ranking, comparison, cross-school total or financial data.
- **Reuse:** each entry calls the existing GROUP report and ATTENDANCE report unchanged for a custom period equal to the stint (so effective start and every counting rule is theirs); nothing is recomputed.
- **Files:** XLSX one worksheet per section (Groups taught, Teacher attendance, Attendance, Memorization, Revision, Evaluations, Exams (counts)), a row per stint with the school, group and dates first; CSV one table (`section, student, group, item, measure, value`) with a short `Teacher` section first, the school and dates in the item column. Both hold every entry of the authorized schools, not just a JSON page. PDF remains deferred.
- **Additive read-only contract in a completed module:** Module 20 `GroupService.listGroupsTaughtByTeacherForReport({ teacherId, schoolIds, from?, toExclusive? })` (+ `GroupRepository.listTeacherTimelinesForReport`, in-memory and SQL; pure domain functions `teachingStints`, `clipStint`; types `GroupTeacherTimelineQuery/Row/Event`, `TaughtGroupStintDto`). One SELECT over `school_groups` and `group_history` restricted to the given schools; no permission, route, event or migration. No contract was added in Modules 27, 28, 29 or 30.
- **Module 38 additions:** `dto/teacher-report.ts`, `ports/teacher-report-sources.ts`, `services/teacher-report-builder.ts`, `PublicTeacherReportSources` (public contracts of Modules 16, 18 and 20 only), `ReportService.teacherReport` / `teacherReportPage` (the `teacher` dependency is optional and also needs `group`), `teacherReportTables` and `teacherReportCsvTable`, three routes, OpenAPI operations `getTeacherReport`, `downloadTeacherReportCsv`, `downloadTeacherReportXlsx` and the `TeacherReport` schema, composition root `src/config/report-modules.ts`. Earlier report behavior was not changed.

No existing test was edited apart from that conformance file's operation count and the harness wiring.
