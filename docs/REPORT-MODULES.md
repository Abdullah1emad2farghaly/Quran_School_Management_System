Module 38 is a read model: it owns no table, no migration and no permission. Every report is computed inside the requester's reporting scope (Module 37, `docs/REPORTS-CORE.md`) from the owning modules' public contracts.

## Owner decisions that shape this module
- 2026-11-17: the recommended availability matrix of `REPORTS-CORE.md`; exports (PDF, XLSX, CSV) approved in principle.
- 2026-11-18: authorized to build; report contents are **data-only** (counts and lists from existing records; no invented scoring, grades or rankings); exports **CSV + XLSX + PDF**; built in **slices**.

## Every report follows the same chain
1. `ReportScopeResolver.authorize(requester, type)`: the ACTIVE role may run this report type and has a current, non-empty scope, else 403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`.
2. The period is resolved by Module 37 (`THIS_MONTH`, `LAST_MONTH`, `ACROSS_TIME`, `CUSTOM`), else 400 (`INVALID_REPORT_PERIOD` and friends).
3. Requested filters are checked with `restrictToScope` / `authorizeRequestedIds`: 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN` for anything outside the scope (existence is never revealed).
4. Only then is data read. The requester comes from the verified session (`req.auth.userId` and `req.auth.activeRole`), never from the request.

A CSV or Excel file is built from the same report object as the JSON response.

## Slice 1: PAYMENT report
`GET /api/v1/reports/payment` (JSON), `/payment/csv`, `/payment/xlsx`.

| Query | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `period` | required: `THIS_MONTH`, `LAST_MONTH`, `ACROSS_TIME`, `CUSTOM` |
| `startDate`, `endDate` | `CUSTOM` only, `YYYY-MM-DD` (Africa/Cairo) |
| `schoolId` | optional, narrows to one school inside the scope |

Rows, one per school and billing month (sorted by school name, then month), all counts:
`paid`, `unpaid`, `waived` (subscription statuses now, Module 33), `subscriptions` (their sum), `paymentsRecorded` (successful payments that stand, Module 34), `paymentsReversed` (reversed payments, kept as history). Plus `totals`. No amount: the system stores none (§60).

Who: ORGANIZATION_MANAGER (schools of their organizations), SCHOOL_MANAGER (schools they actively manage), SUBSCRIPTION_COLLECTOR (schools they are currently linked to). Everyone else is refused.

**Interpretation to confirm:** a billing month is included when it lies inside the period (the months of the period's first and last business dates, inclusive). `ACROSS_TIME` has no bounds. The subscription status counts are the CURRENT statuses of those subscriptions.

CSV: UTF-8 with a byte order mark (Excel reads Arabic), CRLF, headers in the request language (Arabic by default), text that starts with `= + - @` is neutralized against spreadsheet formula injection. Excel: one worksheet, bold header, Arabic sheets right to left, text always written as text.

## Slice 1b: ATTENDANCE report
`GET /api/v1/reports/attendance` (JSON), `/attendance/csv`, `/attendance/xlsx`. Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), and the optional narrowing filters `schoolId`, `groupId`, `studentId`.

Rows, one per school, group and subject (sorted by school, group, type, name): `subjectType` (STUDENT or TEACHER), `subjectId`, `subjectName`, `schoolId`/`schoolName`, `groupId`/`groupName`, `present`, `absent`, `total`. Plus `totals` for students and for teachers separately. Counts only: no rate, grade or ranking (owner decision: data-only).

- **Group at the time (§65):** the group of a row is the group stored on the attendance record. A student who changed groups has one row per group.
- **Student effective start (§67):** a student's rows start on the later of the student's join date and the period start; records before the join date are never reported, even with `ACROSS_TIME`; a student who joined after the period ended has no rows. The join date comes from Module 19 (`listReportFacts`); the report re-reads only the students whose first record precedes their start.
- **Who:** ORGANIZATION_MANAGER and SCHOOL_MANAGER (students and teachers of their schools), TEACHER (the students of the groups they currently teach), PARENT (their own children). Everyone else is refused.
- **Filters:** a requested school, group or student must lie inside the scope: an Organization or School Manager's group or student is checked through Modules 20 and 19 (their school must be in the scope); a Teacher's student must currently be in one of the groups they teach; a broader level than the role's own (a school for a Teacher, a school or group for a Parent) is refused. All are checked BEFORE any attendance is read.
- **Interpretations to confirm:** (1) teacher attendance rows are shown to managers only (a Teacher or Parent never sees a teacher's attendance); (2) a Teacher sees the whole history of the groups they currently teach, even before they became their teacher; (3) the report is not paginated (rows = schools x groups x subjects in scope).

## Slice 2: MEMORIZATION report
`GET /api/v1/reports/memorization` (JSON), `/memorization/csv`, `/memorization/xlsx`. Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), and the optional narrowing filters `schoolId`, `groupId`, `studentId` (the same as the ATTENDANCE report).

Rows, one per school, group and student (sorted by school, group, student): `studentId`, `studentName`, `schoolId`/`schoolName`, `groupId`/`groupName`, and four counts. Plus `totals` of the same four counts.

| Count | Meaning (all stored by Module 27 when each record was made) |
|---|---|
| `fulfilled` | the recorded range is exactly the assigned range (`matchesAssignment = true`) |
| `notFulfilled` | a different range was recorded against a scheduled assignment (`matchesAssignment = false`) |
| `unscheduled` | nothing was scheduled for the student in that session (`matchesAssignment = null`) |
| `records` | `fulfilled + notFulfilled + unscheduled` |

Counts only (owner decision: data-only): no score, grade, weighting, rate, ranking or completion rule, and no number of ayahs (overlapping and repeated ranges are valid in Module 27, so summing ayahs would double count; no such figure is invented).

- **Group at the time (§65):** the group of a row is the group stored on the memorization record. A student who changed groups has one row per group.
- **Period of a record:** a record belongs to the Africa/Cairo business day of its session's scheduled start (`session_scheduled_start`), the same instant Module 27 snapshots for ordering. The period bounds come from Module 37 as the half-open UTC interval `[start, endExclusive)` (§66, §71).
- **Student effective start (§67):** a student's rows start on the later of the student's join date and the period start (computed by `ReportPeriodService.studentWindow`); records before the join date are never reported, even with `ACROSS_TIME`; a student who joined after the period ended has no rows. The join date comes from Module 19 (`listReportFacts`); the report re-reads only the students whose first record precedes their start.
- **Who:** ORGANIZATION_MANAGER and SCHOOL_MANAGER (students of their schools), TEACHER (the students of the groups they currently teach), PARENT (their own children). SUBSCRIPTION_COLLECTOR, MAIN_ADMIN, GOVERNORATE/CENTER/VILLAGE managers and STUDENT stay denied (`REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`); the availability matrix of Module 37 was not changed.
- **Filters:** a requested school, group or student must lie inside the scope, checked through Modules 20 and 19 (an Organization or School Manager) or through the Teacher's current groups before any record is read; a broader level than the role's own (a school for a Teacher, a school or group for a Parent) is refused (`REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN`).
- **Corrections:** a corrected record counts once, with its CURRENT stored fulfilment state; the original stays in Module 27's correction history (nothing is read from it).
- **Pagination (standard list pagination, Master Specification: default 20, maximum 100; Module 00 `PAGINATION`):** the JSON report is ONE PAGE of rows. Query `page` (default 1) and `pageSize` (default 20; above 100 is capped at 100, not rejected); not a positive integer: 400 `INVALID_PAGE` / `INVALID_PAGE_SIZE` (same rule and codes as every list). The envelope carries `meta: { page, pageSize, total, totalPages }` where `total` is the number of rows of the WHOLE report; `data.totals` always cover every row, never just the page. A page beyond the last is an empty `rows` list. CSV and Excel are complete files (no pagination): an export is the whole report. No approved exception for reports was found in the specification, REPORTS-CORE.md or the earlier decisions, so the standard applies. The report is still built for all rows in memory and then cut (the SQL already returns one aggregated row per school/group/student/state, not one per record); a page is a response-size limit, not a database cursor.
- **Interpretations to confirm:** (1) a record is placed in the period by its session's scheduled start, not by the moment it was recorded; (2) a Teacher sees the whole history of the groups they currently teach, even before they became their teacher (as in the ATTENDANCE report); (3) the three fulfilment counts are the only measure: no ayah total, no percentage; (4) the CSV and Excel files are not paginated.
- **Not changed here:** the PAYMENT and ATTENDANCE reports (verified) are still unpaginated; the standard applies to them too unless you approve an exception, which needs your decision because it changes verified API behavior.
- **Query performance (reviewed, no migration):** `memorization_records` has one index useful here, `ix_memorization_record_student` (student, session start), so student-filtered and Parent reports are indexed. School-wide and group-wide reads (Organization/School Manager, Teacher) filter by `school_id` or `group_id` plus the session start and `GROUP BY` school, group, student, state: there is no usable index for them (the table's indexes are `ix_memorization_record_session`, `ix_memorization_record_student` and `ix_memorization_record_assignment`, and the migration declares no foreign keys on `school_id`/`group_id`), so they read the whole table. There is no measured problem and no data to measure on; a migration is NOT justified yet. Decision rule: if `EXPLAIN` on production-size data shows a scan, add one composite index `(school_id, session_scheduled_start)` (a new, non-destructive migration) after your approval; the single-database test suite cannot show this.

CSV and Excel use the same table as the other reports (headers in the request language; Arabic by default).

## Slice 4: EVALUATION report
`GET /api/v1/reports/evaluation` (JSON, one page of rows), `/evaluation/csv`, `/evaluation/xlsx` (complete files). Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), the optional narrowing filters `schoolId`, `groupId`, `studentId`, and for the JSON `page`/`pageSize` (standard pagination: default 20, maximum 100, `meta`, `totals` over all rows; the same rules and codes as the MEMORIZATION report).

Rows, one per school, group of the evaluation and student: `evaluations` (how many evaluations, one per student per session, in the period) and `scores`, the four categories of Module 29 (`MEMORIZATION`, `REVISION`, `PERFORMANCE`, `TAJWEED`), each `{ count, average }`: `count` = how many evaluations have a score in that category (a student only has the categories available to them), `average` = the arithmetic mean of those stored scores (0.00 to 10.00) rounded half up to two decimals, `null` when `count` is 0 (never a made-up zero). `totals` = `evaluations` and `scores` over EVERY row; each total average is computed from the exact sum of all the scores (kept as integer hundredths, so there is no floating-point drift), never as an average of the row averages.

- **Data only; what was NOT invented:** no combined or overall score across categories (that would be a weighting nobody approved), no grade, rate, ranking, trend or completion rule, and the Teacher's note is never included (a per-session text, not a figure). The only calculation is the per-category arithmetic mean (see the interpretations below).
- **Group at the time (§65), period, student effective start (§67):** exactly as in the MEMORIZATION report. An evaluation belongs to the Africa/Cairo business day of its session's scheduled start (`evaluations.scheduled_start`, the snapshot Module 29 stores).
- **Who:** ORGANIZATION_MANAGER and SCHOOL_MANAGER (students of their schools; they see every Teacher's evaluations), PARENT (their own children, all their evaluations) and TEACHER (see below). SUBSCRIPTION_COLLECTOR, MAIN_ADMIN, the area managers and STUDENT stay denied (`REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`); the availability matrix of Module 37 was not changed (EVALUATION was already allowed for these roles).
- **Teacher:** Module 29 decision 2 says a Teacher reads the evaluations of sessions assigned to them only. Only the session's Teacher can record an evaluation, so the report restricts a Teacher to the evaluations RECORDED BY the requester (`evaluations.recorded_by` = their user id), within the groups they currently teach. A Teacher therefore does not see an evaluation another Teacher recorded for a student in the same group.
- **Filters and corrections:** as in the MEMORIZATION report (proven inside the scope before anything is read; a broader level than the role's own is refused). Module 29 keeps NO history: a score change overwrites the score, and the report counts the current scores.
- **Interpretations to confirm:** (1) the per-category count and arithmetic mean are the report's only figures (the owner's data-only decision does not name averages; a mean is the least interpretive summary of scores, but it is a calculation, so it is listed here); no combined score across categories; (2) the Teacher's note is excluded from the report; (3) a Teacher sees only evaluations they recorded (Module 29 decision 2); (4) an evaluation belongs to the period of its session's scheduled start; (5) the CSV and Excel files are not paginated.
- **Query performance (reviewed, no migration):** `evaluations` has `uq_evaluations_session_student` and `ix_evaluations_student` (student, scheduled start); `evaluation_scores` has the primary key (evaluation, category) and a foreign key to `evaluations`. Student-filtered and Parent reads are indexed. School-wide, group-wide and Teacher reads filter by `school_id`, `group_id` or `recorded_by` plus the start and have no dedicated index, so they read the whole table; the report runs two grouped queries (one over `evaluations`, one joining `evaluation_scores`). Nothing is measured and no migration was added; if `EXPLAIN` on production-size data shows a scan, a composite index such as `(school_id, scheduled_start)` would be a new non-destructive migration after your approval.

## Slice 5: STUDENT report
`GET /api/v1/reports/student` (JSON), `/student/csv`, `/student/xlsx` (complete files). Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only) and **`studentId` (required)**; for the JSON also `page`/`pageSize` (standard pagination: default 20, maximum 100). The report is about ONE student. Owner-approved on 2026-11-21 (all eight recommendations of the pre-implementation questions, with clarifications).

Seven data-only sections:
1. **Attendance**, 2. **Memorization**, 3. **Revision**, 4. **Evaluations**: exactly the ATTENDANCE, MEMORIZATION, REVISION and EVALUATION reports for that student, obtained by calling those reports unchanged (same counts, same effective start, same Teacher restrictions), one row per group of the record (the group at the time, §65) plus totals. Nothing is recomputed, so a figure can never differ from the dedicated report.
5. **Exam results**: one row per exam in which the student has a RECORDED outcome: `examDate` (the exam's start date, Africa/Cairo, which decides the period), `outcome` (`EXAMINED` or `ABSENT`), the group at the time, `questionCount`, `maxScorePerQuestion`, `scores` (the recorded score of each question, in order; EMPTY for ABSENT), `total` (the recorded total; null for ABSENT) and `maxTotal` (the exam's own maximum). `counts` = `{ examined, absent, total }` over EVERY exam of the report. Nothing is summed, averaged or compared across exams; no percentage, grade or rank. This is the ONLY paginated part (`meta` = `{ page, pageSize, total, totalPages }` for the exam list); every other section is returned whole. Oldest exam first.
6. **Subscriptions**: ONLY for ORGANIZATION_MANAGER and SCHOOL_MANAGER (for a Teacher or a Parent the key `subscriptions` is absent, nothing is read, and the files have no such sheet/rows). One row per billing month (`YYYY-MM`) that lies inside the period and is not before the student's join month, with the status recorded NOW (`PAID`, `UNPAID`, `WAIVED`) and `statusAsOf` (when it was read): it is not a historical snapshot. Totals count the months by status.
7. **Group history**: the student's group periods (Module 20 assignment history) that overlap the effective window, clipped to it: `from` (never before the effective start), `to` (the end of the assignment, or the last date of the period for an assignment still open, or null when the period has no end), `current` (the student is still in that group). A Teacher only sees the groups they currently teach.

`student` = `{ id, name, schoolId, schoolName, joinDate }`; `effectiveStartDate` = `MAX(join date, period start)` (§67), null when the student joined after the period ended (every section is then empty and nothing is read). The student's lifecycle status is not part of the report.

- **Who:** ORGANIZATION_MANAGER and SCHOOL_MANAGER (a student of their schools), TEACHER (only a student currently in a group they teach, and only those groups in EVERY section, including exams and group history), PARENT (own children). SUBSCRIPTION_COLLECTOR, MAIN_ADMIN, GOVERNORATE/CENTER/VILLAGE managers and STUDENT stay denied (403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`); the Module 37 availability matrix was not changed (STUDENT was already allowed for these roles). A student outside the scope and a student that does not exist both give 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN`, before anything is read. No Module 12 permission; Module 33's authorization is not widened.
- **ARCHIVED and INACTIVE students** can be reported (Module 19 returns them whatever their status; the report only reads and never changes a status). A manager can always report them. A Teacher's access follows "currently in a group they teach" and a Parent's follows Module 19's list of their children; those two lists were not exercised for archived/inactive students, so if one of them excludes such a student that role gets `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN` for it. (Read in the code for 0.58.0, not run: Module 19's child list of a Parent has no status filter unless one is asked for, so a Parent's scope includes archived and inactive children; the Teacher rule is unchanged.)
- **Files:** XLSX has one worksheet per section the requester may see (up to seven; the first column of every sheet is the student; each sheet ends with a totals row; the exam sheet has fixed `Question N` columns). CSV is ONE table with the columns `section, student, group, item, measure, value` (one row per measure; the first rows are the student's own details, section `Student`). Both hold the COMPLETE report (every exam). PDF stays deferred.
- **Interpretations to confirm:** (1) only RECORDED outcomes are listed: an exam in which the student's outcome is still pending (an exam in progress) has no row; (2) an exam belongs to the period by its START date (Africa/Cairo), not by the date the result was recorded; (3) `maxTotal` is shown for ABSENT rows too (it is the exam's own maximum, not a score); (4) the Evaluations section keeps the EVALUATION report's rule that a Teacher sees only the evaluations they recorded; (5) subscription months are those of the period's first and last business dates (as in the PAYMENT report), starting at the join month; (6) the CSV layout above (long form, because a CSV has no sheets) and the XLSX layout; (7) the period is resolved by each of the reused reports and by the student report itself, so a request made exactly at a Cairo midnight or month change could see two different "now" values; (8) `package.json` version is unchanged (0.1.0).
- **Query performance (reviewed, no migration):** the new reads use `exam_students.ix_exam_students_student` and `subscriptions.uq_subscriptions_student_month` (student-led); the learning sections use the existing indexes described in the earlier slices.

## Slice 6: PARENT report

Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), `parentId` and for the JSON `page`/`pageSize` (standard pagination of the CHILDREN: default 20, maximum 100). Owner-approved on 2026-11-22 (all five decisions). It is ONE Parent and ALL their linked children (§68: "separate context").

- **Content:** `parent` = `{ id, name, children: [{ id, name }] }` (the Parent's name and the list of EVERY authorized child, never a phone number, an address or a lifecycle status) and `childReports` = for each child of the requested page, that child's COMPLETE STUDENT report (slice 5: attendance, memorization, revision, evaluations, exam results with every exam, group history and, for the two managers only, subscriptions). Each child's report is produced by the STUDENT report itself, called unchanged for that child with the same requester and period, so every figure, every effective start and every authorization rule is the STUDENT report's (nothing is duplicated or recomputed).
- **Who:** ORGANIZATION_MANAGER, SCHOOL_MANAGER and PARENT (the Module 37 availability matrix, unchanged); Teacher, Collector, MAIN_ADMIN, geographic managers and Student: 403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`. A manager MUST send `parentId` (otherwise 400 `REQUIRED_FIELD`); a Parent may omit it (their own record, derived from the verified account) and may only send their own id (any other: 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN`). No Module 12 permission and no Module 17 permission is added or bypassed.
- **Scope proof (before any child is read):** a manager's Parent must have at least one child in a school of the manager's scope (organization administrator: the schools of their organizations; School Manager: the schools they actively manage) or be currently linked to one of those schools; otherwise 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN` (the same answer for a Parent that does not exist). A manager only ever sees the children in the schools of their scope, even if the Parent also has children elsewhere. A Parent sees all their own children (also those that Module 37's scope for the Parent role lists).
- **Children:** all the Parent's children of ANY lifecycle status (ARCHIVED and INACTIVE included, status never exposed), ordered by name then id. The report never changes a child.
- **Pagination:** only the children (`meta` = `{ page, pageSize, total, totalPages }`, `total` = the number of authorized children); each returned child's sections, including all its exams, are complete.
- **Files:** XLSX has the STUDENT report's worksheets (up to seven; no subscriptions sheet for a Parent), each holding the rows of EVERY child (first column = the child; each totals row says whose; the exam sheet has as many `Question N` columns as the child with the most questions). CSV is ONE table with the STUDENT columns `section, student, group, item, measure, value`: a short `Parent` section first (the Parent's name and each child), then every child's rows. Both files hold EVERY authorized child, not just a page. PDF stays deferred. For a Parent with no child the file has the sheets' headers and no rows (no subscriptions sheet).
- **Interpretations to confirm:** (1) a manager's Parent is "in scope" when the Parent has a child in the scope OR is currently linked to a school of the scope (so a Parent registered but without children yet is reachable, as in Module 17); (2) the Parent's own children are the intersection of Module 19's list and Module 37's scope list; (3) the JSON returns each child as a full STUDENT report (so `period` and `generatedAt` repeat per child); (4) a Parent account without any child has no reporting scope at all in Module 37 (403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`, unchanged); (5) ordering of the children is by name (locale comparison) then id.
- **Performance (reviewed, no migration):** the children come from the existing `students` Parent queries (no new SQL); each child is reported with the STUDENT report's reads (see slice 5), one child after the other; a page holds at most 100 children.

## Slice 7: GROUP report
`GET /api/v1/reports/group` (JSON), `/group/csv`, `/group/xlsx` (complete files). Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), **`groupId` (required)** and for the JSON `page`/`pageSize` (standard pagination of the STUDENTS: default 20, maximum 100). Owner-approved on 2026-11-22 (all six decisions, with clarifications). The Master Specification only names this report (§68); its content is the owner's decisions below.

- **Header:** `group` = `{ id, name, schoolId, schoolName, learningMode, status, teacherName }` (the current Teacher's NAME only, never a phone number).
- **Roster:** every student assigned to the group at any time inside THEIR effective window (§67: MAX(join date, period start)), taken from the authoritative assignment history (Module 20; never rebuilt from the current roster), with or without activity records. Students who left the group, and ARCHIVED or INACTIVE students, appear for the time they were in it; no lifecycle status is shown. A student whose effective window is empty (joined after the period) is not on the roster. Ordered by name then id.
- **Student rows** (`students`, ONE PAGE of the roster; the only pagination): `attendance` (present, absent, total), `memorization` and `revision` (records, fulfilled, notFulfilled, unscheduled), `evaluations` (the number of evaluations and, PER CATEGORY, the number of stored scores and their mean, exactly as the EVALUATION report defines them; no overall score) and `exams` (examined, absent, total: recorded outcomes of the exams of this group inside the student's window, counts only, no exam score). The first four are exactly the ATTENDANCE, MEMORIZATION, REVISION and EVALUATION reports called unchanged for the group (same counts, same authorization, same Teacher restrictions); a student without records shows zeros (never an invented value).
- **Totals** (`totals`, over the COMPLETE eligible roster, never the page): `students` (the roster size), the sums of the integer counts of every row and, for the evaluation means, the EVALUATION report's own totals (computed from the stored scores; averages are never averaged). Nothing is compared between students; no ranking, weighting or combined score exists anywhere.
- **Teacher attendance** (`teacherAttendance`: present, absent, total): the group's Teacher attendance from the existing attendance records, for the ORGANIZATION_MANAGER and the SCHOOL_MANAGER only. The existing ATTENDANCE report shows a Teacher students only, so for a TEACHER the key is absent (not an invented zero).
- **Financial data:** none, for any role (no subscriptions, payments or fees).
- **Who:** ORGANIZATION_MANAGER (a group of the schools of their organizations), SCHOOL_MANAGER (a group of the schools they actively manage), TEACHER (only a group they CURRENTLY teach). The Parent, Collector, MAIN_ADMIN, geographic managers and Student stay denied (403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`); the Module 37 availability matrix is unchanged. The group is proven inside the scope before anything is read; a group outside it and one that does not exist are the same 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN`. No Module 12 permission is added.
- **Files:** XLSX has one worksheet per section (Attendance, Memorization, Revision, Evaluations, Exams (counts) and, for the two managers, Teacher attendance), a row per student of the COMPLETE roster and a totals row. CSV is ONE table with the STUDENT columns `section, student, group, item, measure, value`: a short `Group` section first (name, school, learning mode, status, teacher, period, number of students), then every student's rows, the totals (student column = Total) and the Teacher attendance (managers). Both hold every eligible student, not just a JSON page. PDF stays deferred.
- **Interpretations to confirm:** (1) a student counts as "in the group during the period" when one of their assignment periods overlaps their own effective window (an assignment ending exactly at the window start does not overlap); (2) the evaluation means of the totals are the EVALUATION report's totals for the group (a record of a student not on the roster, which cannot normally exist, would still count there); (3) exam counts use the exam's start date and the group the student was targeted in (as in the STUDENT report); (4) a Teacher gets no Teacher-attendance section because the ATTENDANCE report hides it from a Teacher; (5) the learning-mode and status values are shown as their codes (`GROUP`/`INDIVIDUAL`, `ACTIVE`/`ARCHIVED`).
- **Performance (reviewed, no migration):** the roster is one read over `group_students` by group (`ix_group_students_group`, filtered by the period); the four learning figures are the existing group-filtered reports (one read each); exam counts use Module 30's per-student read, ten students at a time (one read per student of the roster). A very large group's report costs that many exam reads; a group-level exam read in Module 30 is the optimization to ask for if it ever matters.

## Slice 8: TEACHER report
`GET /api/v1/reports/teacher` (JSON), `/teacher/csv`, `/teacher/xlsx` (complete files). Query: `period` (required), `startDate`/`endDate` (CUSTOM only), **`teacherId` (required)** and for the JSON `page`/`pageSize` (standard pagination of the TAUGHT GROUPS: default 20, maximum 100). Owner-approved on 2026-11-23 (Option A, with the mandatory multi-school scope rule). The Master Specification only names this report (§68); its content is the owner's decisions below.

- **Scope, the central rule:** the report is limited to the REQUESTER'S AUTHORIZED SCHOOLS, never the Teacher's whole employment. The Organization Manager's authorized schools are all the schools of their organization(s); the School Manager's are the schools they actively manage (all of them, never one at random). Every read of group data is given ONLY those school ids (the contract refuses to read any other school), so a school, group, attendance, student or statistic of another school, or of another organization, is never read, counted, paginated, listed, named or exported, and its existence is not revealed (header, groups, `meta`, XLSX, CSV). A School Manager of school A sees only school A even when the Teacher also works in B and C; each manager is independent.
- **Who:** ORGANIZATION_MANAGER and SCHOOL_MANAGER only (the Module 37 availability matrix, unchanged). A Teacher cannot run it, not even for themselves; the Parent, Collector, MAIN_ADMIN, geographic managers and Student: 403 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`. No Module 12 permission added.
- **Scope proof (before any report data is read):** the Teacher must be currently linked to an authorized school OR have taught a group of one at some time (so a Teacher who has left that school keeps their history there). A Teacher outside that and a Teacher that does not exist give the same 403 `REPORT_SCOPE_FORBIDDEN`; the Teacher's name is read only after the proof.
- **Header:** `teacher` = `{ id, name, schools }`: the name and the authorized schools the Teacher is CURRENTLY linked to (a school that is history only is not listed there; it appears through its groups). No phone, no address.
- **Taught groups (`groups`, ONE PAGE; the only pagination):** one entry per STINT: a continuous time the Teacher taught a group of an authorized school, derived only from the group's authoritative history (Module 20): the first teacher recorded at creation, every `TEACHER_CHANGED`, and the archiving (an archived group is not taught). Never from who owns the group now. A Teacher who left a group and came back has two entries for it. Each entry: group, school, learning mode, `taughtFrom` / `taughtTo` (the stint clipped to the period, both days inclusive; an open stint ends today) and `stillTeaching`.
- **Figures of an entry (Option A), limited to exactly the stint's dates:** the Teacher's OWN attendance (present, absent, total: the Teacher's own rows of the existing ATTENDANCE report for the group) and the GROUP report's figures for the group (`students` = roster size, and the students' attendance, memorization, revision, evaluation (per-category count and mean, no overall score) and exam counts (counts only)). Both are the existing reports called unchanged for a custom period equal to the stint, so effective start (§67) and every counting rule is theirs. No score, ranking, weighting or comparison between teachers, groups or schools; nothing is totalled across groups or schools (each entry carries its own school); no financial data. Option B (counts of what the Teacher personally recorded) is NOT built.
- **Files:** XLSX has one worksheet per section (Groups taught, Teacher attendance, Attendance, Memorization, Revision, Evaluations, Exams (counts)), a row per stint whose first columns are the school, the group and the dates (no totals row). CSV is ONE table with the STUDENT columns `section, student, group, item, measure, value`: a short `Teacher` section first (name, the authorized schools the Teacher is linked to, period, number of entries), then, per stint, one row per measure (the `group` column holds the group, the `item` column holds the school and the dates, the `student` column is empty). Both hold EVERY entry of the authorized schools, not just a JSON page. PDF stays deferred.
- **Interpretations to confirm:** (1) the stint's dates are whole business days (Africa/Cairo): on the day a teacher is replaced both the outgoing and the incoming teacher's stints include that day; (2) a group the Teacher left and came back to is two entries (so each entry's figures describe one continuous time); (3) an archived group's stint ends at the archiving; (4) the group's current name and learning mode are shown (the history does not keep the old ones for this purpose); (5) `taughtTo` of an open stint is today's date and `stillTeaching` says it is open.
- **Performance (reviewed, no migration):** one history read per call, restricted to the authorized schools (`school_groups` by school, `group_history` by group through `ix_group_history_group`), read twice (once for the scope proof over all time, once for the period); only the requested page of stints is computed (two existing reports per entry: the GROUP report, which itself reads the roster, the four learning reports and one Module 30 read per student, and the ATTENDANCE report). A page holds at most 100 entries; the files compute every entry.
